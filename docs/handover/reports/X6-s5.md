# X6 단계 5 (AI 의 대응) — 덩어리 작업자의 보고

워크플로 package-run.js 의 구현 덩어리 보고를 그대로 옮겼다(브랜치 wf/catastrophe-s5, 마지막 커밋 14fa1cc, 관문 통과). 정본은 docs/specs/X6-catastrophe.md 끝의 구현 메모다.

## 끝낸 것

- Stage 5, commander rules: new file src/sim/field/orders/catastrophe.ts. Contains DANGER_RULES (a table R3 can add rows to, each with an optional id) and dangerMap, which returns hits plus raw levels. Danger levels 1–3 come from the commander's beliefs and memory only. Also implemented here: zone sync (new or raised zones get a withdrawal order; lowered or vanished zones go through the §5.7 release conditions, plus the 180-second cap for level-2 zones); the withdrawal order with the air horn (reaches every active agent within 28 tiles, even in radio shadow), the WITHDRAWAL record and the head count (resend 3 times, 70-second deadline, downed agents dropped, 'incomplete' message at the end); switching to and from exterior defence (records limitBonus +150 once); limited rescue entry (40 seconds at level 2, risky flag, up to 3 times per zone); DANGER_SIGN (recorded and recovered per zone key); prevention (crack and valve priority raised to 0.92/0.93, cooling, +0.12 to suppression near gas plus fire, gas re-measurement, a single city-gas shut-off request); backup requests and sitreps; and registerGate.
- Stage 5, ops.ts: newOps, bumpZones and zonesVer, zoneMask, zoneLevelAt, regionLevel, regionAt, reachMask and regionReachable (the spec's cutOff), ALLOWED2, targetTile, gasPropKnown, gateBlocks, opsExterior, opsCordon, nearBuilding, evacPoint, exteriorSpots, EXT_RANGE and structScene. NO_STRUCT moved here from catastrophe.ts, which re-exports it. ops.ts still reads no truth (checked by the test).
- Stage 5, gate: sceneRegistry gets WORK_GATES and registerGate. In commander.want, the gate is checked just before a new item is created and returns the frozen GHOST (id -1) when blocked. syncBoard writes the block count to s.ops.gated at the end. The work literal gets risky: undefined. Also in commander.ts: opsCordon added to the cordon condition, a new police request reason, needFor returns null for gas re-measure, and assign skips agents who are evacuating.
- Stage 5, agents: in agents.ts, agentCost treats tiles of a level-3 zone in the agent's belief as impassable (except while evacuating, on a risky task, carrying someone, or standing inside), and avoid tiles cost +20. fitFor adds the gate safety net, avoid, risky, scba required for rescue work in level-2 zones, cooling, gas re-measure (meter holders only), and rescue skill ≥0.3 for a trapped mate. workLabel covers cooling and gas re-measure. hazardSign turns hazard claims in briefFacts into unverified signs. In brain.ts, signDanger is added after the composure multiplier. The evacuate hold and the evacuate duty (evacDuty) sit after the injured-retreat hold and before the R3 doctrine duty block. avoid lasts 30 seconds.
- Stage 5, executor: new case 'evacuate' (runEvacuate: goes to evacPoint and quietly sends the head-count reply ops 100+rid on arrival). Exterior suppression (runExterior and exteriorFire; spray gains eff and wmul arguments, 0.7 and 1.4 here). Cooling of gas cylinders and drums (runCool: reportDone if already exploded; on finish, learnQuiet of vessel sign 0 with down set). Gas re-measure branch in mitigate. Rescuing a trapped mate was already in place from stage 3; fitFor now gates it.
- Stage 5, dispatch: the sitrep branch of dispatcher.fieldReport raises fieldRisk to the next severity threshold, adds the risk and re-assesses, but only when the severity name or risk list actually changes. briefUpdate now accepts the hazard topic. analyst.assess merges fieldRisk. markRecoveredBy added to events.ts. createIncident literal gets fieldRisk: undefined. types.ts TaskKind gets 'evacuate'.
- Stage 5, display: catLayer draws no-go zone tape (alternating two-colour solid line) and the labels '진입 금지', '철수' and '외부 방어', the same in both views. Evacuating agents' name labels come from the task label '철수'.
- Stage 5, tests: tests/catastrophe.test.ts now has 74 tests. The stage-2 shift group's 'nobody decides yet' check is flipped to 'records match command state and stats'. New groups: §10-4 (withdrawal, horn, shadow, head count, defensive mode, corridors), gate / move cost / pulling back, and prevention / gas / dispatch.
- Checked that R3's doctrine.opsLevel matches the real ops fact shape (OpsFact zones[].regions/level); no change needed.

## 설계와 다르게 한 곳

- Names: the spec's cutOff, exteriorSpot, opsOf and inZone became reachMask/regionReachable, exteriorSpots (returns a candidate list), direct reads of s.ops, and zoneLevelAt/regionLevel. NO_STRUCT moved to ops.ts and catastrophe.ts re-exports it. GHOST is a frozen WorkItem with kind 'sizeup', status 'cancelled', id -1. DangerRule has the shape {id?, run(cx, out)}, and dangerMap returns {hits, raw}.
- Exterior suppression in a level-2 zone (not defensive mode): if no outside spot can reach the fire and the region is reachable, the agent suppresses from inside, since that is allowed work at level 2. Otherwise a fire in a windowless room inside a level-2 zone could never be put out.
- noExt only blocks while opsExterior is true for that region, and the entry is dropped once it is not. The spec says 'while exterior defence lasts, clear on interior'. The memory recorded in a level-3 zone outside defensive mode outlived the zone's release and blocked suppression forever (S11 #01 stalled 74 s and hit the time limit).
- opsCordon only applies to scenes that have s.spots. Fire and collapse scenes built by the plan generator have no spots, so the perimeter task repeated 'no entry route' dozens of times (S7 #15).
- The gas level-3 self duty does not apply to agents doing work allowed at level 2 (ALLOWED2: mitigate, suppress, carry, extricate, waterRescue, rescueMate). Without this, meter carriers sent to shut the valve walked out as soon as they read the gas level, and gas explosions over 12 shifts went from 0 to 5.
- a.avoid is set only for sign-driven retreats and self-duty evacuations. The spec says 'any retreat'; applying it to ordinary fire retreats would upset fire balance, and failedAt already does that job.
- The horn merges the ops fact straight into each agent's beliefs instead of using learnQuiet, so it does not go into every agent's outbox and get re-broadcast.
- If the commander is in radio shadow and the order cannot go out, a level-3 zone with agents inside is not created at all; it is reconsidered next cycle. A zone with nobody inside is created without an order.
- DANGER_SIGN is keyed by zone key and raised when the raw command-side danger level (before release and limited-entry overrides) reaches 2, not the sign level. It recovers after 20 s at ≤1, or on gas source cut-off. It stays unrecovered if a collapse or explosion happens at that spot (for the building key, any partial collapse counts). After a recovery it is only raised again once the level has dropped below 2.
- The WITHDRAWAL record carries text '… — 인원 점검을 시작합니다.' because EmitArgs requires text. The head-count resend is a single order radio message ('인원 점검: n명 미확인 — … 응답하라.') rather than a separate record plus a resend.
- Sitreps go out on collapse/explosion events, on switching to defensive, and on interior → withdraw. fieldReport only re-assesses when the severity name or risk list changes. fieldRisk is merged right after the severity clamp in assess, before needs are computed.
- burnSince and everHot are only recorded on real building scenes (structScene).
- Cooling is not re-issued for 30 s after a completed cooling unless the vessel sign is ≥2. Without this, a cooled drum was re-assigned 17 times.
- Limited entry sets the zone's level directly to 2. While the entry is active, dangerMap also overrides level 3 to 2. When it ends the zone goes back to 3 and the order is resent; a second WITHDRAWAL record is not made if an unrecovered one with the same detail exists.
- The evacuate move pushes through fire, like retreat: moveTo(…, 1.6, false, true).
- Zone release rules: struct releases immediately if it has collapsed, otherwise after 40 s at a lower wanted level; gas releases immediately; vessel releases immediately at 0 and steps 3→2 after 20 s; fire/dust release immediately; building releases after 40 s at 0, and the stable rule lowers it to 2 immediately.
- The city-gas shut-off request is a radio message to dispatch (to −2, kind 'request'); nothing is added to s.requests. Conditions: (a) a level-3 zone covers a known active leak in a region with a gas sign, or (b) a verified gas sign ≥2 exists and no haz leak is known.
- targetTile also covers the perimeter's exit tile, so circle zones that cover an exit are respected.
- Rescue work inside a level-2 zone (carry, extricate, waterRescue, rescueMate) requires an scba holder. Body condition is not checked, as the spec says for before R3.
- bumpZones also discards the mask cache for that zones array. Before this fix, a zone raised from level 2 to 3 was still computed as level 2.

## 설계서 밖에서 고친 공용 파일

- src/sim/traffic.ts endTraffic (new) and src/sim/engine.ts checkShiftEnd (one call before ended = true): clears event-crowd congestion (bump) that is still active at shift end. This does not change results (nothing runs after the shift ends, and the report reads only traffic stats). Reason: tests/disaster.test.ts 'congestion is all cleared' failed for DZ-1 typhoon/quake. Because of X6, the traffic picks shifted and a crowd started 40 s before the end, leaving 0.8 congestion.
- src/sim/field/commander.ts assign: no orders to agents who are evacuating. Not in the spec's file list. Without it, orders sent while an agent was evacuating ended as 'no response' (COMM_FAIL).
- src/sim/field/catastrophe.ts: NO_STRUCT moved to ops.ts and re-exported (X6's own file; noted for the record).
- src/sim/dispatch/dispatcher.ts briefUpdate: accepts the hazard topic and forwards it via hazardSign (§3.6). Listed in the spec, recorded here because it edits a shared file.

## 알려진 문제 — 다음 작업자가 알아야 할 것

- New stall path: after a withdrawal or exterior defence, nobody goes back in. It does not show in the gate (normal 40 seeds: 0), but forced runs produce STALL with 'open work 0'. A level-3 zone remains (usually the 260-second strong-fire row or the whole-building zone). Exterior suppression fails twice for a room no outside spot can reach, so noExt blocks it. The commander's fire belief stays stale, canClose stays false, and the incident runs to the time limit. Stage 6's exterior-confirmation close (§6.2), end blocker and limit-time bonus are the spec's way out. Examples: forced fire/std S3 #11, S3 #06; collapse/gasblast S15 #02.
- A commander who arrives after a total collapse never hears the collapse event. They only see the building sign 3 ('건물 전체 붕괴 — 잔해 더미'), so whole-building exterior defence starts and never ends (forced fire/std S3 #11). Stage 6's rubble search opens on event total + 30 s, so it needs a path that works without that fact, e.g. have perceiveSigns teach the event fact to an agent who sees the collapsed building.
- Time-limit closes grew: normal +4, hard +29, extreme +50. This is outside the spec §8.1 target of '+2 at most' for normal. The fix is stage 6's limit-time bonus (s.cat.limitBonus + s.ops.limitBonus, both already recorded) and its endings.
- Balance is stage 8: fire-caused partial collapses are +97% over baseline; averted signs 32% (target 60%); hazmat gas explosions near 0 (target 5–15%); the 'two struct-3 regions → whole building' rule fires often on hard and extreme.
- Fire and collapse scenes have no s.spots, so opsCordon and the police request do not fire there. If spots are added later, the perimeter will work.
- The ops fact is broadcast silently with 'brief'. When the commander is in shadow it waits for the next cycle; the commander's own belief is updated straight away.
- The horn Fx 'horn' is now recorded, but sound, camera and auto slow-down (DECISIVE, ACTION_W, legacy) are stage 7. The UI does not error on it.
- N8 prep workers are running on the same machine. I merged the main branch when I started (it had not moved). The next worker should merge it again first.
