## 설계와 다르게 한 곳

- perceiveSigns has no throughWalls argument; its signature is (sim, s, a, v). External observation does not change sensing: sound already passes through walls and the eye condition uses line of sight. extSeen is maintained by perceive.
- judge has an 'upper bound when there is no observation' extension. Read literally, §3.1 rule 1 means an agent outside the region who hears the imminent-collapse sound stop learns nothing. §5.7 says this can be confirmed from outside, so if the sensing condition of a level above the truth is met, the level just below it is reported as a down report (e.g. 3 → 2 '붕괴 임박 징후 멎음').
- The thermal camera's 'burning region' means fire ≥ 0.3 OR already-burned tiles. With fire alone, the threshold flipped between 0.7 and 0.6 as fire moved in and out, and the creak / no-problem radio repeated every 6 seconds.
- The eye condition for struct level 2 is only evaluated when the truth or the agent's previous observation is ≥ 2; the outcome is the same. Sensing conditions are passed to judge as an array (COND) rather than closures.
- Wording for drop reports (structure, building, vessel, gas), the outside text '○○ 외벽 균열 — 붕괴 임박!', and bldg fact x,y (building centre, region −1) are not in the spec and were decided here.
- The gas meter calls kitWorks on every reading while hasKit is true and there is a region to read. gasMeter is not a faultable item, so in practice no fault is ever revealed.
- extSeen is updated on every throughWalls observation, even when the tile-scan block is skipped.
- AI-view drawing (signs the commander knows) is included already in stage 2. The G4 gas drawing (gasView) is left as is; stage 4 switches it to c.gas.
- scripts/metrics.ts and tests/causality.test.ts needed no changes: the new radio events are kind 'radio' and do not match PHRASE.
- Name / signature notes: vesselLevel's first argument is s, not c (vessel heat lives on the prop).

## 알려진 문제 — 다음 작업자가 알아야 할 것

- Stage 3–4 workers should know that two parts of the test file get flipped: the second test in 'X6 단계 2: 교대 — 재현성과 아직 일어나지 않는 일' (nothing is triggered yet), and in the hand-calculation group '방치된 화재 … 한 칸도 다르지 않다', '단계 1 은 터뜨리지 않는다' and '기록은 아직 없다'.
- structLevel returns 0 for a collapsed region. An agent nearby who knew it was 'imminent' then reports a down to 0 with the text '구조 이상 징후 없음', which reads oddly. After stage 3 adds collapse events, consider skipping collapsed regions inside perceiveSigns.
- In the observer view, X6's c.gas contours and G4's smoke-based gas outline (dashed, '가스 고임 — 폭발 위험') both appear. Stage 4 should switch G4's gasView and hazardChips to c.gas.
- Vessel signs are rare at this stage: the old rule explodes cylinders instantly at heat around 0.2. This changes after stage 4. Balance warnings ① and ② from stage 1 still stand (collapse/gasblast has no cloud in the neglect test).
- COMM_FAIL goes up because level-2+ signs count as important; this is per spec. In extreme, problem counts in reports rise by about 13%.
- The scratchpad is session-local. If it is missing, unpack 313ec41 with git archive and re-measure using the tools described in the memo; the scripts themselves are also in the scratchpad, so they would need rewriting.
- This branch now includes N8 prep 1 and 2a merged from main (05d136d) as well.
