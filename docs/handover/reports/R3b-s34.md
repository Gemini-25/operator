- S30 conditions are stricter than the spec. The spec's: armed scene, after the entry decision, gun threat in belief, locked door in belief, current task not engage or force. Added: kits ['charge']; own reported injury band 0; also skips arrest and clear (S30_KEEP); does not fire during a sub-task; door must border the believed gun suspect's room within 8 tiles (DOC.S30_NEAR); an engage teammate within 6 tiles (DOC.S30_TEAM) and nobody already opening the door. The last three came from measurement (Q45 #9, S35 #10).
- The new DocActId line is named 'force'. docBegin uses the act name directly as TaskKind, so the executor's existing case 'force' runs it. The 60 s door memory ('S30', 'd:<tile>') is checked inside breachDoorOf rather than pickAct's memRule, which keys on the threat, not the door.
- The 8 s wait is checked after the opener candidates are gathered, and skipped if a charge-carrying breacher is already among them. breacherOn also counts an engage breacher on the same suspect who is on the way (forced hostage S31 #3: the SWAT leader opened while a breacher 9 tiles away was still coming).
- D2 'no casualty in the room' counts every live casualty, held or not, including the door tile. The hostage branch counts only casualties not held.
- Tests for D1, D2 and D4 are all in tests/methods.test.ts (the spec allows either file). D2 'hash unchanged' is shown as 'shifts with no hostage-free barricade hash the same with solo on or off', since old code cannot be compared inside a test.
