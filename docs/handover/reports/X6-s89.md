## 설계와 다르게 한 곳

- The spec says to judge balance on 60 normal shifts. Here 60 shifts are too noisy: with the same constants, S0–59 vs S60–119 gave burnout 0/172 vs 9/155, total collapses 2 vs 10, partial collapses 36 vs 56–59. So knob decisions used 120 shifts (S0–S119, x6scen.ts), with the baseline re-measured on the same seeds (old ceiling collapses 36 + 39 = 75). sweep 60 numbers are recorded alongside.
- The '다 탄 듯한 건물' rule thresholds moved from the spec text (50%/120s) to 40%/90s. These are the §8.4-2 knob, now named ENGULF_T / ENGULF_SHARE in orders/catastrophe.ts.
- GASBLAST_INIT (the stage-4 initial gas, which §2.5 says to tune) 1.0 → 1.3. §8.4-4's GAS_SRC is very steep (0.15 → 6.8%, 0.2 → 27% with that scenario's success −25 points), so it stays at 0.15.
- Dust ignition is one roll per second: a new collapse gives 0.5, otherwise a new impact within 4 tiles gives 0.5, and the impact left by the same collapse is not rolled again. Then heavy fire rolls 0.04. The spec lists the three ignition sources separately. Dust detail text: '분진 폭발 — {점화원}' with three ignition-source phrases.
- The dust region only needs to be indoor; c.struct is not required. Flashover applies only to indoor regions of kind 'room' (halls are also 'room'). Collapsed rooms and rooms that already flashed over have rollover truth level 0.
- The rollover and dust sign eye condition is the same check as structure level 2 (seesRegion): within 6 or 5 tiles, in line of sight, covering 30% of the region's tiles. The spec says 'the room's tiles are in view'. Down-report texts are not in the spec and were decided here. The dust region is drawn as a single label, not hatching.

## 알려진 문제 — 다음 작업자가 알아야 할 것

- Do not judge X6 balance from sweep 60 alone — use 120+ shifts (x6scen.ts 0–120). With 60 shifts, burnout looks like 0%.
- Three targets pull against each other: averted signs 60%, withdrawals 4–15, and fire-caused partial collapses at baseline ±30%. Fire-weakened rooms below 0.36 can never recover, so fixing one breaks another. Whether to restate the §8.1 targets is for the documentation stage or the user. The cylinder target (25–60%) rests on the wrong premise that cylinders 'currently explode almost 100%'; the old rule was also 28%.
- One stall candidate remains in forced runs (S2 #14, an ordinary fire that appears in both the fire/plane and armed/bomb shifts). After building sign 3 the crew waited 134s outside for the total collapse, a tail case of the 2%/s collapse roll. Raising TOTAL_BASE would shorten it. The normal 40-shift gate is unaffected.
- Hard timeouts 3 → 5 and extreme 12 → 17 vs baseline (sweep 60).
- Exterior-confirmation close is still 0 events, as noted in stages 6·7.
- Dust and flashover are off. Turning dust on: start by lowering ignition source ③ (heavy fire, 0.04/s) or DUST_P. Turning flashover on: lowering the probability won't help; the fire-share condition has to change. N9's fallback ('if X6 stage 9 is missing, plantRule does one secondary explosion') still applies.
- The main branch has newer commits than 3de4f50, which I merged at the start. The next worker should merge it first.
